The main feature of a person born on the day of the Dog is the rare vividness and strength of his emotions. His feelings are so powerful that it is they that shape his entire character, his bearing and his way of life. This by no means implies that logic is foreign to him: on the contrary, the Dog has a very quick mind, he grasps new things on the fly and has an excellent memory. It is just that his thinking is itself colored by emotion. Dry, dispassionate reasoning is not for him, and he often trusts his inner instinct more than rigorous calculation. A telling example is Bobby Fischer, one of the strongest chess players in history, who was born under this sign. No one ever managed to beat him. And yet, for all his intellect, Fischer was extremely emotional and constantly found himself at the center of scandals of every kind.

Because of this heightened emotionality, the Dog person often keeps something childlike for his entire life. He frequently retains a sincere capacity for wonder and joy into deep old age. By nature he is a romantic who cannot sit still and who is forever drawn to new adventures. This pull can show up in any area of his life.

Like any temperamental person, the Dog finds the gray routine of daily life not merely oppressive – it can even undermine his health. After all, the fuel for his vitality is feelings and emotions, and they need a constant supply of novelty. Without it they simply go out, and extinguished emotions mean, for the Dog person, a loss of vital energy – and then he risks sliding into deep depression more than anyone else. At the same time, most people born under this sign know or intuitively sense this trait in themselves, and so they seek out on their own the adventures that bring the necessary novelty into their lives.

The craving for adventure shows up in full in the Dog person's love life as well. Being sincere by nature, he is not inclined to infidelity, yet reining in his sensuality can be hard for him. This, however, does not at all mean that his only option is to seek variety on the side. Often, rather than break themselves, such people prefer to agree honestly with their partner on an open relationship, though even this is far from the only way out. Here it is important to remember a simple rule: if you want to keep a Dog person by your side, never, under any circumstances, let your love turn into routine – otherwise he may cool off sharply and quickly. Try to make your relationship an exciting game. Do not be afraid to tease and provoke him. In short, treat him like a big child, so that his feelings are constantly stirred up. And rest assured: then the sincerity and devotion characteristic of the Dog will reveal themselves in all their strength.

Much the same holds for work. The Dog person is a romantic and a dreamer, and, believe me, the last thing he wants is to spend his life in a dusty office shuffling papers on a desk. Even if he himself assures you that such a job is the height of his dreams, do not believe him: it is nothing but bravado. Their liveliness of feeling and restlessness push people of this sign toward occupations connected with creativity or adventure. For example, one of the most prominent representatives of the Dog sign chose the profession of intelligence officer while still young – a trade steeped in romance. Today his name is known throughout the world: Vladimir Putin.

Among the traits of the Dog sign is also a gift for eloquence. A conversation with someone born on this day is hardly likely to bore you: on the contrary, thanks to his temperament he can liven up any discussion, whatever the subject.

The Dog has, perhaps, only two flaws. The first is a lack of restraint, which may concern anything: a fondness for good food, for carnal pleasures or for drink. Once carried away, these people often lose all sense of measure. The second is that their stormy emotionality frequently becomes a source of conflict. People of this sign pay little attention to what they say and may allow themselves overly harsh words that wound the other person. In short, it is hard to suspect them of an excess of diplomacy.

All this, of course, complicates life for someone born under this sign, so if he wants a more fortunate fate and career, he should learn to keep under control, if not his feelings, then at least his actions and words. Then his many talents, his ability to learn quickly and to grasp the heart of a matter at once will surely secure him a worthy position in life, while his vivid, lively emotions and sincerity will unfailingly draw many friends and allies to him.
