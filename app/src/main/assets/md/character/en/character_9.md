Water is one of the most contradictory signs, and for that reason it is often misread. A person born on this day really does have such an unusual temperament that those around him constantly find his behavior strange and devoid of logic. But that impression is misleading. The truth lies elsewhere: Nature has invested the Water person with enormous inner reserves, yet putting them to use in everyday life is extraordinarily hard for him – they kick in only in critical situations.

Hence the consequence: in calm times, a person born on this day often comes across as slow-witted, a dawdler, even a loafer. But when a hard stretch arrives, an uncommonly powerful energy suddenly awakens in him – to the astonishment of everyone, himself included – and it is this energy that leads him out of the dead end. That is the moment when it becomes clear in what way the Water person surpasses everyone else.

Think of the Russian epic about Ilya Muromets, the folk hero who sat on the stove for thirty years and three. Only when disaster struck and Rus fell under the yoke of invaders did he suddenly acquire the strength of a bogatyr, a legendary warrior, and, as if by magic, clear his native land of the enemy. The Water person is built the same way. There is never a shortage of hardship in his life, but heavy karma has nothing to do with it. Nature has simply assigned people of this sign the role of humanity's emergency reserve: in dark times it is they who must take on what nobody else can handle.

In everyday terms it looks like this: a person born on the day of Water is often surprised to discover how hard the most elementary things are for him – things everyone else manages with ease. Take, for instance, the habit of going to work every day and always keeping busy with something useful. For most people this is as natural as breathing – but not for the Water person. Such simple rules cost him far too much effort, he quickly runs out of steam, and so he often simply ignores generally accepted norms.

Small wonder that with this approach, everyday troubles rain down on the Water person one after another, as if from a hole in a sack. But here lies the paradox of his nature: those very same troubles mobilize him and make him show miracles of quickness and energy. In other words, he has a unique gift for dealing remarkably well with the problems he himself has created. Curiously, knowing this trait in themselves, people of this sign sometimes create difficulties for themselves quite deliberately! For example, the painter Salvador Dalí, born under this sign, was utterly incapable of working in a calm setting. In order to paint, he deliberately wore himself down with lack of sleep – only in that half-fainting state were his world-famous canvases born.

At the same time, although he himself tends to be unhurried under ordinary circumstances and does not pay much heed to rules, he often expects the exact opposite from others. At times the Water person can be quite strict with those around him, without noticing the beam in his own eye.

That strictness, however, like his whole manner of behaving, is hardly consistent. When he is in a benevolent mood, he can turn a blind eye to other people's slips and weaknesses for a long time, and then suddenly present them as an accusation and demand that everything be put right at once. Understandably, this style of communication does not always get results.

The Water person is by no means indifferent to wealth in any of its forms. But whereas others need prosperity as a means of obtaining life's comforts, what he holds dear is the very idea of accumulation. Here he can be utterly insatiable. And yet, curiously, he hardly cares about putting what he has accumulated to sensible use. Often he simply enjoys owning the most seemingly worthless objects, justifying it with a simple motto: "it might come in handy."

This indiscriminate collecting can extend to anything – from stockpiling useless junk to the haphazard hoarding of information from the most varied fields. But here is the remarkable thing: much of this stockpile – objects and information alike – really does turn out to be useful at the most unexpected moment, for few people can match his knack for turning junk into something worthwhile at the right minute by dreaming up unusual uses for things. In short, if there is a wizard in this world capable of making gold out of garbage, he was surely born under the sign of Water.

With money, too, the Water person is often inconsistent. In business dealings he can be tight-fisted, even stingy, but at times he is capable of astonishing those around him with an unexpectedly awakened generosity. On the whole, though, the urge to set something aside for the future prevails in him – and he often succeeds at it quite well. Thus it was under Bill Clinton, a representative of the Water sign, that America's wealth reached the highest mark in the country's entire history.

Naturally, from the outside (and often in his own eyes as well), the Water person appears a highly contradictory figure. His behavior can be irritating in its inconsistency and lack of logic. And yet it is wrong to be seriously angry with him: this temperament was placed in him by Nature herself, and she has special plans for him. He simply was not created for the sake of the most comfortable life possible, but so that in the critical hour he could bring his colossal inner reserves into play, solve the impossible task and rescue everyone else.
