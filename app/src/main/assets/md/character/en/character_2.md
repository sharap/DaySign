People of the Wind are as contradictory as the element itself. A primal, untamed energy lives in them – and yet it is not the blind pressure of nature but a force aware of itself.

The Wind person is endowed with a very strong consciousness, including a clear sense of his own "I". His mind never stops for a moment. The state of happy, thoughtless contemplation is almost unknown to him – on the contrary, his mind is always busy with something. That does not mean he is forever absorbed in weighty problems: like everyone else, he thinks about everything under the sun, trivia included. But the point is that in his head even a trivial thought gathers quite noticeable force. In people of other signs such pressure might grow into an obsession, but for the Wind it is his native element: he is used to it and therefore copes fairly easily with the powerful current of his own consciousness.

The mind of the Wind is nothing like the mind of a level-headed scholar, where everything is sorted onto its shelf and every item has a place assigned once and for all. His reason is more like a wild mountain torrent that knows no barriers.

The chief feature of the Wind person's consciousness is the naturalness of his thinking. His mind may not be especially orderly, but deep intuition more than makes up for that shortcoming. He is not inclined to analyze a situation at length; a decision usually comes to him suddenly – and then nothing on earth can stop him.

The Wind's philosophy is not the abstract reasoning of a dry-as-dust scholar: his consciousness is set in motion by Life itself, and so it is inseparable from it. He was born, if not to unravel nature's innermost secrets, then at least to touch them.

The Wind person cannot stand templates that force thought into rigid frames. Just as a real wind cannot blow forever in one direction, so a person born under this sign cannot live without a sense of freshness. That is why his ideas and actions always bear the stamp of the novelty and surprise he needs so much.

The power of the Wind rewards those born on this day with a rather difficult character. His independence, his taste for authority and his peremptory manner, which often borders on intolerance, make the people around him keep their guard up.

His behavior, like his thinking, can be unpredictable. From the outside it may seem that his interests are far too varied and replace one another far too quickly, but that is an inherent trait of every free-thinking person. On the other hand, his turbulent energy and untamable consciousness let him infect others with his ideas and emotions and lead them along – vivid examples are the electrifying dancing of Michael Jackson or the irresistible smile of Adriano Celentano, both of whom were born on the day of the Wind.

The sign's chief flaw is perhaps that the Wind person gives no thought to the possible ill consequences of his steps and believes so firmly in himself and his own powers that he may simply fail to notice other people's opinions. Those around him usually take this for self-assurance and egocentrism – which, in essence, is exactly what it is. Still, the Wind is not much more of an egocentric than anyone else: it is just that, because of the power of his thinking, the trait shows up more noticeably and vividly in him. It would do him no harm to be more circumspect and to remember that now and then others, too, can say something worthwhile.

Along with an untamable consciousness, the sign of the Wind gives those born on this day an equally untamable sexual temperament. His appetites in love can be truly impressive, but that by no means dooms the Wind to inconstancy. To bring a touch of novelty into his feelings he has no need to change partners: the Wind's fantasy is so strong that the play of his own powerful imagination is often all he needs.

One more trait of Wind people is their craving for inner freedom, and open space is their native element. Whatever those around him may think of the Wind person, his inner freedom, sincere feelings and brilliant mind leave no one indifferent. And one thing can be said for certain: no one will ever be bored at his side.
