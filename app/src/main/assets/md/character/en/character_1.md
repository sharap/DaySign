A person born on the day of the World Tree is inseparably bound to the deep forces of nature. He may not notice this bond himself, yet it is there and stays with him all his life. His intuition is extraordinarily well developed, and he is used to relying on it far more than on the arguments of reason. He lives in natural harmony with the world around him, and though his actions are often far from logical, people are drawn to him with an inexplicable liking – precisely because of this organic, unforced naturalness. In this he resembles an overgrown child: he can be hard to deal with, but it is just as hard to stay angry with him for long.

The comparison with a child is no accident: the first nine years of anyone's life, and the very first year in particular, pass under the powerful influence of this very sign. So if you want to understand the World Tree better, imagine yourself as an infant. When a baby is hungry, thirsty or bothered by something, he does not weigh the circumstances – he simply demands what he wants, loudly. Of course, an adult, whatever sign presided over his birth, has a far more complex mind – and yet the habit of putting his own desires and feelings above everything else stays with the World Tree to the end of his days.

If someone born on this day wants something – say, he is hungry or feels attraction – he does not devise elaborate plans but heads for his goal by the shortest and most natural road. And when no such desire is present, there is hardly a force in the world that could make him do what he does not want to do.

From the outside it often looks as though the World Tree person throws himself into something in an instant and cools off just as quickly. But that is not quite accurate. If he has lost interest, it means the desire has been satisfied for now; as soon as the need returns, so will the interest – with all its former force. For the same reason he is quick-tempered but quick to calm down: nursing a grudge for long is simply not in his nature.

In calm, prosperous times people of this sign are usually at peace with the world around them; they feel no urge whatsoever to act and contentedly watch events unfold. But touch a raw nerve, and all their elemental power bursts out at once – and for that power, no obstacles exist.

The reason is that primal instincts are the spark for all our feelings and thoughts, the loftiest and the basest alike, and the stronger those instincts are, the harder they are to keep in check. Just try carrying on a leisurely polite conversation while gripping a red-hot frying pan! Something similar is what World Tree people, with their mighty instincts and emotions, go through when an unresolved problem is lodged inside them.

On the other hand, that same strength of feeling can find an outlet in creative work, and the World Tree's intuition and naturalness can only be envied.

All this speaks of the sign's dual nature. It is important for someone born on this day to learn to channel his enormous elemental power into constructive ends – otherwise it may carry him off to who knows where. On the whole, the sign is beautifully suited to any creative pursuit that calls not for cold calculation but above all for intuition, depth and spontaneity of feeling.

The World Tree person is extremely susceptible to outside influences of every kind, good and bad alike: he readily soaks up the shared emotions and moods around him. For such an impressionable and unrestrained nature, the false blessings of civilization such as alcohol and drugs can pose a considerable threat. A heavy, hostile atmosphere weighs on him as well, and it noticeably tells on both his mood and his health. Hence a simple piece of advice: to restore their emotional balance, people of this sign should get out into nature more often – to places where the world is not yet buried under the refuse of civilization and life is as harmonious and natural as they are themselves.
