In the Maya language the word OC literally means "step," "path," "course," though it may also be translated as "wayfarer," "wanderer": thus the Maya expression "ah oc" translates as "one who walks." Among the K'iche' Maya the same sign is called Tzi, and it has several meanings – "dog," and beyond that "willful" and "imprudent." The Maya glyph for the sign Oc itself depicts a dog's head with a stylized paw print inscribed inside it. This drawing should be read as a hint at restlessness, a craving for adventure and wandering.

Often the Oc glyph shows a chain of black dots drawn around the dog's muzzle – this is how the idea of talkativeness and heightened emotionality is conveyed. The "Book of Omens of the Days" points to the same thing, naming the magpie among the omens of this day.

Thus the sign embodies inquisitiveness and curiosity combined with heightened emotionality.
