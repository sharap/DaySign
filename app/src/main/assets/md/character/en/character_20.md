People born on the day of the Lord possess the secret of an inexplicable magnetism. They are almost never alone: others are drawn to them, and a circle of company is forever forming around them. What attracts people to the Lord are such qualities as self-confidence, a love of life and an open, easy manner.

At the same time, the inner balance of someone born on this day in no way implies passivity. On the contrary, he is a very active person with strong emotions, which he knows how to manage with real virtuosity. Nature has given him an enormous reserve of active, creative energy, and thanks to it he is capable of achieving a very great deal.

As a rule, the Lord's career goes well. He is comfortable in positions of leadership, he knows how to set up a business of his own, he does not take unjustified risks and therefore rarely makes serious mistakes. More than that, even after a major blunder he has the ability, as the saying goes, to come out of the water dry.

The Lord is usually authoritarian by nature, but in him, unlike most other signs, this trait does not stick out or push people away. He can indeed be very demanding, insistent and even tough, yet he does it carefully enough not to provoke active resistance.

The Lord has an astonishing gift for leading people and organizing them around himself. Often he does this so subtly and unobtrusively that his role as organizer remains entirely invisible, in full keeping with the old Chinese saying: "the best ruler is not the one who is loved, but the one whom nobody notices". The people around him may believe that they are making their own decisions and acting in their own interests, yet in some mysterious way it is always the Lord who ends up at the center, with events revolving around him. It is simply that nature has endowed him with a masterful command of emotions, his own and other people's. Intuitively, without even thinking about it, he finds precisely the words and the manner that stir the strongest feelings in people.

Those feelings can be of any kind. Depending on the circumstances, the Lord can inspire, he can outrage, or he can defuse the atmosphere with an unexpected remark and put out a conflict before it flares up. And he switches from one emotion to another so quickly that serious clashes with him are uncommon.

This is precisely what his organizing talent consists of. His method is not to stifle emotions but to find a counterweight to them in other emotions. As quickly as the Lord can provoke displeasure, he can, when necessary, extinguish it just as fast by turning the other person's attention to something else. It happens that after a quarrel the Lord will start talking to someone again as if nothing had occurred, and he does it so sincerely that not a shadow of the recent tension remains. That is why it is hard to stay angry at these people for long, and they themselves usually stop holding a grudge just as quickly.

There is, however, a flip side to this sign. By no means every emotion in life can be balanced out, and when that happens the Lord risks losing control of the situation. This weakness usually makes itself felt in his attitude toward health, his own or that of those close to him. Here an excess of emotion can work against him.

Of course, everyone knows a touch of hypochondria, but in the Lord it can reach extreme intensity. Unlike others, he is not used to suppressing his feelings, so when he feels anxious about his health (as everyone does sooner or later) and cannot find a way to put things right quickly, he is more likely than anyone to give in to panic. At such a moment the Lord needs a doctor who will not only treat him but, above all, calm him down and instill faith in a speedy recovery.

In this connection it is worth noting that nature has given people of this sign an exceptionally strong immune system and a sturdy, healthy body that anyone might envy. So if the Lord ever does seriously undermine his robust constitution, it will be first and foremost through his own hypochondria and needless worry.

Another characteristic feature of the Lord is a well-developed mind. Strange as it may seem, for all his emotionality he manages in most cases to remain a remarkably sober-minded and even calculating person. He thinks with great logic, and strong feelings do not get in the way of his thinking at all.

It is no accident that Albert Einstein, the greatest genius of the modern age, whose very name has become a byword, was born on the day of the Lord. No one could call Einstein dispassionate: one need only recall the world-famous photograph in which the great scientist, without the least embarrassment, sticks out his tongue. According to the recollections of his contemporaries, he had an enviable sense of humor, could put a person in his place with a single well-aimed phrase and was, on the whole, a man of very strong feelings. And yet, with a temperament like that, few have managed to leave a mark on science as bright and as significant as his.

Overall, the influence of the Lord sign on people is powerful and organizing; nature itself has arranged things so that people are instinctively drawn to them. The Lord is an organizer by calling, and it is well within his power to live a long, happy and successful life, especially once he learns to make sensible use of his remarkable abilities and does not squander them on trifles.
