Yet, this same strength becomes a weakness when real problems arise: she often avoids confrontation or practical solutions, burying her head in dreams until the emotional pressure becomes unbearable. However, her intense sensitivity means she can be deeply affected by negativity, and she may withdraw or escape into fantasy when overwhelmed. Her ideal partner is someone who respects her need for space and emotional honesty, and who can gently guide her back to reality when her dreams become too consuming. She excels in artistic or creative fields—writing, painting, music, or any form of expressive work—where her intuition, emotional depth, and ability to find magic in the ordinary can flourish.
