Those who come into the world on the day of the Thunderstorm have been dealt a genuinely hard lot. People of this sign feel everything that happens to them with extraordinary intensity; their nerves are nearly always stretched to the limit, and although they grow used to this constant tension as a kind of background noise that accompanies them from birth to old age, it breaks out into the open fairly often. Yet there is another side to it: no other sign has given the world so many celebrated people whose mark on it has been so bright and so deep. In this respect the Thunderstorm is far ahead of every other sign in the Maya calendar.

A person born on this day is emotional to the very core. He may look calm on the outside, but his bearing, and above all his eyes, betray a tremendous force of feeling and a hidden tension. He resembles the sky before a storm, ready at any second to come down on the earth in a wall of rain or to light up with lightning. This is true of every feeling the Thunderstorm person has, the joyful ones and the dark ones alike. What is more, his moods replace one another with astonishing speed. It is said that there is only one step from love to hate; for the Thunderstorm it is not even a step but half a step, so quickly does he swing from wild delight to wild fury and back again.

None of this, however, means that in everyday life people of this sign are completely out of control. On the contrary, they are well aware of their deep emotionality and understand perfectly where it can lead. As a rule, they learn from their own experience already in childhood that their powerful, unbridled impulses have to be held back, and they do everything in their power to master themselves. The trouble is that with a temperament like this it does not work every time, and now and then the feelings spill over anyway. Such is their nature, and they cannot change it.

This unending struggle with his own powerful emotions tempers the will and builds a tenacious character, but it also lends the Thunderstorm person a certain shade of tragedy. Indeed, someone who is forever at war with himself is hardly likely to be carefree and cheerful, which is why the face of a person of this sign is most often serious, and quite frequently visibly tense.

The picture is complicated still further by such Thunderstorm traits as independence and willfulness. With feelings of such force, he is simply incapable of adapting to circumstances or submitting to other people's orders.

Naturally, living side by side with someone so independent, whose feelings are so deep and so unpredictable, is very hard. And the stormy temper of the person born on the day of the Thunderstorm creates a mass of difficulties for him as well: his whole life is made up of strong emotions and passions. On the other hand, wherever sheer power of feeling is exactly what is required, no one can compete with the Thunderstorm. It is wonderful when such people find themselves in the arts, for it is there that their enormous potential unfolds best of all.

Moreover, his temperament constantly lands the Thunderstorm in the very thick of scandals and sensational events, which means that his chances of ending up on the front pages are higher than anyone's. Curiously, the founder of the "yellow press" itself, the kind that lives on scandal and scandalous rumor, is considered to be the famous American journalist Joseph Pulitzer, and he was born on the day of the Thunderstorm.

Here lies the principal gift of the Thunderstorm person: the ability to draw attention to himself. He does not even need to get mixed up in scandalous affairs to do so; his remarkable personality attracts attention all by itself. The secret is that his whole appearance, and his eyes above all, carry a certain mesmerizing force, like the air before a storm.

It is not only the feelings of the Thunderstorm that are marked by power and depth, but his mind as well. He is, of course, nothing like a dry armchair scholar, but he is able to bring his powerful enthusiasm and inspiration even to scientific work, giving it new and vivid colors. So it is no surprise that this sign has produced quite a few outstanding scientists. It is enough to name James Clerk Maxwell or Niels Bohr, one of the founders of quantum physics.

Another notable trait of the Thunderstorm is that he is far from indifferent to money, fame and all the other worldly temptations. Everyone dreams of fame and wealth, of course, but no one else experiences these desires so acutely. Furthermore, people of this sign are, as a rule, catastrophically bad at being content with what they have: here, as in everything else, they are given to excess and immoderation.

In short, an easy life is not in the cards for someone born on the day of the Thunderstorm. This does not mean that tragic trials are necessarily his destiny; the chief trial of the Thunderstorm is himself and his difficult character. He is like the energy of the atomic nucleus: it can destroy, but it can also be tamed and turned to creation. So if the Thunderstorm person wants to live a full and happy life, it is important for him not merely to keep himself in check, but first and foremost to find inner balance. Then the enormous power of his feelings will surely turn its bright side toward him and lift him to heights that, perhaps, no one else can reach.
