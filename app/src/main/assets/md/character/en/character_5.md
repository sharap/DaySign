Among the Maya, the Snake sign enjoyed special affection and deep reverence. The Indians noticed that the most powerful shamans and seers are born on precisely this day. Curiously, the Bulgarian prophetess Baba Vanga, whose abilities were confirmed more than once by serious scientists, also came into the world under the Snake sign. Of course, the gift of prophecy is by no means granted to everyone born on this day, and yet in the life of each of them there is, one way or another, a special thread linking him to the Divine.

In the Snake person this connection may show itself in different ways – for instance, as unusually vivid dreams or premonitions. Obeying an unconscious intuition, he sometimes does things that look illogical from the outside, and at times even he himself cannot explain the reason. Strange things often happen around a Snake person: life may suddenly lift him to unimaginable heights – and just as easily hurl him into the deepest abyss.

All the more surprising, then, that in everyday life the Snake usually comes across as calm, even-tempered and even a little lazy. The Maya books mention the drowsiness characteristic of people of this sign, but they also say that a fire burns in the Snake's heart – and there come moments when that fire breaks free with incredible force. It is like looking at a hand grenade: it is hard to imagine what terrible power lies hidden inside its casing until someone pulls the pin.

In practice this means that emotions can sweep over the Snake person suddenly and with tremendous force – in love and in business alike. It is precisely this combination of outward calm and unexpectedly erupting, stormy feelings that makes the Snake's character so remarkable.

A person born on this day is, as a rule, able to endure hard trials steadfastly and patiently, yet it does happen that some trifle makes him literally explode (fortunately, this does not happen to him often). Just bear one thing in mind: what seems a trifle to those around him does not necessarily look like a trifle in the Snake's own eyes. It is no accident that the Indians compared this sign to a rattlesnake: under ordinary conditions it is rather lazy, loves basking in the sun and never attacks without reason – but just try tickling it or pulling its tail!

By nature the Snake falls in love easily. His feelings are extraordinarily strong and never pass without leaving a trace. At the same time he is particular in his choice of partner and will hardly agree to settle for a half-hearted relationship. He needs real love – the kind that takes hold of him completely and to which he is ready to devote himself without reserve. And when such love comes, the Snake acts swiftly and without looking back.

It must be admitted that the Snake's character is poorly suited to everyday routine, and he finds it hard to do anything that does not touch him to the quick. A steady working rhythm comes hard to the Snake, for he is used to moving in bursts. He can toil tirelessly for months, astonishing everyone with his inexhaustible energy, and then do absolutely nothing for just as many months. Even if he tries to squeeze his impulsive temperament into the mould of monotonous, assembly-line work, very soon either the assembly line or the Snake himself will give way.

Creative professions suit the Snake far better – literature, music, theatre, film: here his powerful energy can unfold to the full.

Be that as it may, both the person born on this day and those close to him should remember that the Snake's outward calm and composure are deceptive. Nature has simply made sure that his formidable power and enormous energy are not squandered. Like a hand grenade, the Snake person is designed not to advance toward a goal step by methodical step, but to release all his power at once, at the right moment and in the right place. His calling in life is to do the impossible and thereby push evolution forward, and in this sense his drowsiness serves him as a kind of safety catch. After all, if his energy poured out continuously, being near him would be almost impossible, and the Snake person himself would very quickly exhaust his strength.

There is, however, another, deeper meaning to the Snake's characteristic drowsiness: it flows from his special kinship with the Higher powers, for there is no other way to touch the Divine. In the Bible, after all, God addresses Moses with the words: "When there is a prophet among you, I, the Lord, reveal myself to them in visions, I speak to them in dreams" (Numbers 12:6-8). And indeed, any spiritual practice is inseparable from a state of trance, sleep or meditation.

In short, it can be said with confidence that the life of someone born on this day will not be easy, but it will be vivid and full of events. Nature herself has arranged things so that he does not turn into an ordinary cog in someone else's machine. He was born for more.
