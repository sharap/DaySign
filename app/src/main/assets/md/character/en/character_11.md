Nature has been generous to anyone born on the day of the Master: his abilities show up in the most varied fields. What others achieve through stubborn, patient work comes to him easily, almost as if in play. He thinks fast, grasps the heart of a matter in an instant, has rare insight, and his conclusions are usually strikingly accurate. But this very ease conceals a trap – it makes him relax. Quite often a person born under this sign simply has no incentive to put his many gifts to work. The result is a paradox: having received far more from nature than most people, the Master wants to make use of it less than anyone.

People of this sign are often prone to complacency. Somewhere deep down the Master knows perfectly well – or at least senses – that his abilities are out of the ordinary, and that in some respects he outright surpasses those around him. He notices that much in life comes to him without any particular effort, and often that feeling alone is enough for him. A pity, because a gift that goes unused can fade. Just as muscles weaken without regular exercise, so the Master, over time, risks losing his talents or frittering them away on trifles.

The Master may regard his rich gifts in different ways. Sometimes he takes his high abilities for granted and therefore undervalues them. Then he quite sincerely believes that everyone around him is every bit as gifted as he is, while he himself is destined for the lot of an ordinary person who is never going to achieve anything special. Such lack of faith in his own powers really will keep him from serious results – only it will be himself, not nature, that he has to blame.

The opposite also happens: deep down the Master feels that he stands above the rest. He is often irritated when people cannot understand what seems simple and obvious to him. Gradually such an attitude can grow into a rather unpleasant trait – arrogance, or even outright conceit.

The Master's fate turns out best when life itself does not let him sit idle and forces him to exert himself and move forward. Under such conditions he is truly capable of achieving enormous success.

For the famous Hollywood actress Barbra Streisand, who was born on the day of the Master, that push – by her own admission – came from her unremarkable looks. All her life she strove to prove what she was worth, both to herself and to everyone around her. And here is what is remarkable: without a Hollywood smile, without sex appeal, without any apparent chance at superstar status – she achieved that status all the same.

Still, hardship is not the only thing that can drive the Master. Sometimes a strong interest or an absorbing passion is enough to pull him out of his state of contented idleness. That is exactly what happened to Jacques-Yves Cousteau, the famous French oceanographer, also born on the day of the Master: the beauty and mysteries of the underwater world captivated him so completely from childhood that he devoted his entire life to them without reserve. And here the Master's gifts came in very handy indeed. To get closer to his dream, he invented the aqualung along the way – with the same ease, it seems, with which the rest of us pack a bag before a trip.

In short, the abilities of people born on this day are truly impressive. The main thing is to manage them wisely and not let them wither.

In other words, if the Master wants to succeed, he should remember that talent is not everything. It is only an advance issued by nature, and it still has to be earned – his gifts must be put to worthy use. If he manages that and overcomes his own laxity, generous fate will surely throw open every door that leads to success.
