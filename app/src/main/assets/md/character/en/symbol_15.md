Eagle came to this sign from the Aztecs and stuck as its usual name; the K'iche' call it Tz'ikin, "bird". The Maya word proper is MEN — "deed", "occupation", and also "talent", "ability".

A face fills the glyph, an eagle's or a person's, and a line of black dots runs from its eyes back to the brain. Maya pictograms read that as "many-eyed" and "many-wise": someone who notices much and thinks well. Across the top of the head a straight horizontal stroke is often drawn, which in pictograms marks a highest level, the upper boundary of something. Set beside the "many-wisdom", it pushes the reading to its limit.

Men is the sign of intellect at its fullest reach.
