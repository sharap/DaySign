But this very struggle becomes his catalyst: when pushed to the edge, he unleashes an astonishing surge of creativity, determination, and clarity, capable of solving problems that seem insurmountable to others.

He expects loyalty and honesty from others, yet often overlooks his own contradictions and inconsistencies. His moods shift unpredictably—he can be warm and empathetic one moment, then withdraw into silence the next. Yet his sensitivity is not weakness; it is a source of profound wisdom. Like a phoenix rising from the shadows, he can emerge from darkness not just intact, but transformed, with gifts—creativity, insight, and compassion—that few others possess.
