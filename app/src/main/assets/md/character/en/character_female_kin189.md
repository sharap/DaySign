They thrive in creative, intuitive, or healing professions—writers, artists, therapists, or visionaries—where their depth and imagination can shine. While they may seem inconsistent or unpredictable, their actions are often guided by a quiet, long-term vision. They are not driven by external validation but by inner truth and purpose.
