Thus, he benefits most from a calm, reassuring guide—someone who not only treats the body but also restores peace of mind. He is fiercely fair, often driven by moral conviction, and unwilling to compromise his principles, even at personal cost. Yet, his strength lies in his ability to balance firmness with empathy. When he sees injustice, he acts—but not with rage, rather with focused resolve. If he learns to temper his directness with patience, and his independence with vulnerability, he becomes not just a leader, but a true guide—someone who leads not by domination, but by inspiring others to rise.
