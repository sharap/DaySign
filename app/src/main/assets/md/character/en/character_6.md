It is often said that the best defence is a good offence, but this formula does not apply at all to a person born under the Skull sign. Nature has made him so that he is best protected by calm inner firmness, caution and restraint.

By temperament the Skull person is not at all inclined toward aggression. More than that, from the outside he often seems gentle, but do not forget that behind this outward gentleness, and at times a seeming childishness, lie considerable inner strength and a stubborn will.

If, in conversation with him, you suddenly get the impression that he is about to give in or abandon his convictions, do not let his calm mislead you: he will not compromise under any circumstances, and he will yield to crude pressure even less. Of course, like anyone else, he may retreat for a while, but he never regards that as defeat – it is merely a tactical move on the way to his goal. And rest assured: he will manage to turn even a forced retreat into a stepping stone to future victory. In this sense the outlook of Skull people is akin to the ancient Chinese philosophy which holds: "The strong can defeat only the weaker. The gentle defeats all."

Friendliness, outward gentleness and an absence of crude aggression are altogether typical of the Skull, and all this coexists perfectly with his patient, unswerving progress toward the goal he has set himself.

A person born on this day is very patient and endowed with no small will. At the same time he prefers to act cautiously, avoiding abrupt and ill-considered steps: the sign itself nudges him in this direction, quietly reminding him of the possibility of death and of fatal consequences.

The Skull person knows how to wait for the right moment and does not like to hurry. That is why from the outside he often seems slow and not even particularly quick off the mark, but believe me: thanks to his unhurried pace he is capable, in time, of overtaking far nimbler rivals. By his make-up he is not a sprinter but a long-distance runner, and the well-known rule "slow and steady wins the race" suits him perfectly.

That said, if circumstances demand quick action from him, his strong will can come to his rescue here too, although such haste goes against his nature and is unlikely to give him any pleasure.

People of this sign also possess a healthy conservatism. The aim of their life is not to create something new and unprecedented, but to strengthen what already exists as firmly as possible and draw the maximum benefit from it. The Skull person is not inclined to back down or change his convictions, and yet his bearing usually has a certain femininity and smoothness about it – and it is precisely these that often confuse those around him, creating a false impression of weak character. In fact the Skull person has a solid, unbending inner core; it is simply not his way to put his strength on display.

When dealing with a Skull person, one should also take into account his tenacious memory – for good and for ill alike. Do not let his calm and restraint mislead you: he never forgets an injury done to him. This does not mean he will necessarily pay the offender back when the opportunity arises, but in his relations with that person he will always keep what happened in mind.

Incidentally, it is not only his antipathies that are constant: his sympathies are just as steady, as are all his other feelings. That is why, remembering the good, the Skull person can turn a blind eye to other people's shortcomings for a long time, until they cross a certain critical line. And once that happens, have no doubt: his firmness will show itself in full.

One way or another, healthy conservatism makes this sign a fortunate one not only for a steady, fulfilling life but also for achieving success. Inner balance helps those born on this day make fewer mistakes – as the great Confucius said, "The cautious seldom err" – and patience allows the Skull person to scale ever new heights confidently and without fuss.
