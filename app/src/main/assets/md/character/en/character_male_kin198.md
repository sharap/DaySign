A man born under the sign of **Flint** (Kremny) directed toward **Griff** (Gryf) embodies a powerful fusion of inner strength and profound emotional depth. He is a rare blend of unwavering determination and sensitive introspection — a warrior with a poet’s soul. His core identity is rooted in **absolute self-reliance**; he believes in making his own choices, shaping his destiny without compromise. This deep conviction gives him immense personal strength, resilience, and the ability to overcome obstacles others would see as insurmountable. Yet, unlike a purely rigid or detached individual, his resolve is intertwined with a **deep emotional intelligence** — he feels things intensely, not just intellectually, but in his bones. This makes him not only a decisive leader but also someone who deeply understands the human condition.

His **strengths** lie in his unshakable integrity, sharp analytical mind, and the courage to stand by his principles even when it costs him personally. He is direct, honest, and refuses to compromise on truth — a quality that earns him deep respect, even from those who initially find him harsh. He possesses **rare perseverance**, capable of long-term focus and relentless effort toward goals he believes in. His life is often marked by significant achievements, whether in creative, intellectual, or leadership fields — a testament to his ability to turn vision into reality. At the same time, his **empathy and moral sensitivity**, inherited from the Gryf, make him deeply attuned to injustice and the suffering of others. He is not only a fighter for personal success but also a champion of fairness, often drawn to causes that demand courage and moral clarity.

However, his **weaknesses** stem from this same intensity. His **lack of flexibility** can make him appear stubborn or inflexible, especially when others challenge his views. His **bluntness**, while rooted in honesty, can wound others, especially when he speaks his mind without filtering it through diplomacy. His **emotional depth** can also lead to internal tension — he may struggle with overthinking, self-doubt, or an almost obsessive need to "get to the truth" in every situation. His intolerance for hypocrisy or injustice can border on rigidity, and his deep sensitivity to "messiness" — both literal and metaphorical — may manifest in excessive cleanliness or a need to eliminate emotional clutter. In relationships, he seeks authenticity but may struggle with vulnerability, fearing that emotional openness will be misused. Yet, when he does open up, his loyalty and emotional presence are profound.

In his **view of the world**, he sees life as a battlefield of ideals — where truth, justice, and personal responsibility must be fought for daily. He respects strength, integrity, and honesty above all else, and he expects the same from others. He is not interested in superficiality or compromise for the sake of harmony. His ideal relationship is one of mutual respect, deep understanding, and shared values — not one based on comfort or convenience. He thrives when he can channel his energy into meaningful causes, whether artistic, intellectual, or social. With time and self-awareness, he can learn to balance his fierce independence with compassion, his directness with empathy, and his inner fire with emotional grace. When he does, he becomes not just a conqueror of challenges, but a guiding light — a man whose strength is not just in his will, but in the depth of his soul.
