A man born under the sign of **Reed** (Trostnik) directed by the **Serpent** (Zmey) embodies a powerful fusion of relentless growth and hidden, dynamic energy. His core nature is defined by an unyielding drive to expand—professionally, personally, and spiritually. Like a reed breaking through concrete, he possesses extraordinary perseverance and resilience, refusing to accept limits or defeat. Yet, this same energy is channeled through the enigmatic and intuitive essence of the Serpent: calm on the surface, but brimming with deep, untapped power. He is not a steady, consistent worker, but a force of sudden, intense bursts—capable of months of hyper-productivity, followed by periods of deep stillness or even withdrawal. His strength lies in his ability to focus intensely on one purpose, to master a field completely, and to transform small details into meaningful breakthroughs. He sees the world with sharp clarity, avoiding ambiguity, and values truth and precision above all.

His relationships are marked by profound depth and loyalty, but also by intensity. He remembers every slight, every unspoken word, and can harbor feelings for years—making emotional honesty and transparency crucial in his connections. He is deeply empathetic, especially toward children, whom he sees as fellow souls driven by the same unquenchable desire to grow. His love is total and transformative, but he demands the same in return—superficial attachments are unbearable. He is drawn to creative, independent, or spiritually significant pursuits (art, music, writing, psychology, healing), where his bursts of energy can create lasting impact. Yet, he struggles with routine, repetition, or any role that suppresses his inner fire. His quiet demeanor masks a soul in constant motion—connected to unseen forces, guided by intuition, dreams, and sudden insights.

Ultimately, this man is a paradox: a quiet storm. He is both the unbreakable reed that pushes through walls and the sleeping serpent whose power awakens only at the right moment. His greatest strength is his ability to turn passion into mastery and intuition into revelation. His greatest challenge is learning when to rest, when to redirect his immense energy, and how to balance his need for growth with emotional and spiritual equilibrium. When aligned with his true calling, he becomes a transformative force—someone who doesn’t just climb, but reshapes the path itself.
