For those born under the Grain sign, most of the body's strength and resources go into growth and ripening. Physically this shows up as heightened activity in all physiological processes, and in behavior as a hunger for action and a drive to bring one's plans to life.

That is why the Grain person stands out for his high capacity for work and his energy. As a rule he does not set himself the task of inventing something completely new and unprecedented. His main goal is to get the most out of what is already at hand, and here nature itself prompts him to solutions that no one else expects.

Where others see no prospects at all, the Grain person is able to spot hidden reserves and bring his idea to life brilliantly. At times it seems that, like a magician, he creates something useful literally out of nothing, pulling a rabbit out of an empty hat. But that is not quite right. His gift is to breathe life into what looks hopeless to everyone else.

The Grain person is highly observant: he notices small details and can grasp the very essence of a thing on the fly.

The main thing about the Grain person is his practicality and his taste for the concrete; complex abstract philosophies and theoretical schemes are not to his liking. He judges any idea not by its speculative beauty but by how well it can work and what real benefit it will ultimately bring. In this sense he is more a practitioner than a theorist, more an engineer than a philosopher.

People of this sign are not indifferent to worldly comforts and wealth; more than that, nature itself has arranged things so that a large share of their thoughts and feelings revolves around precisely this. But that does not at all mean the Grain person is concerned only with stuffing a sack full of money. On the contrary, his aim is to earn in order to spend, and to spend in order to earn again, and money really does often come to him, as they say, of its own accord. Among those born on this day there are many gifted entrepreneurs and simply good, well-paid specialists. And although people of the Grain sign dislike letting money sit idle for long, let alone saving it for the future, their energy and earning ability usually allow them to live quite comfortably.

The sign does have its flip side, though. Along with energy and observation, the Grain person received from nature a tendency toward jealousy, and it is not confined to love alone – he can be jealous of other people's successes and achievements too. It is no secret that in our world the pursuit of success and wealth inevitably goes hand in hand with envy. By the very makeup of his character, the Grain person is practically doomed to have many around him envy both his talents and his earnings. But he is no stranger to this feeling himself: someone else's success can sting him badly. Of course, such feelings are familiar to everyone, but in the Grain person they can flare up in full force, which is why he, more than anyone, needs to learn to channel this trait in a constructive direction. After all, jealousy always has another side – it can be turned into the healthy excitement of competition and rivalry.

Another trait of the Grain person is a tendency toward excess. He often spends far too many resources on reaching his goals and can, as the saying goes, overdo it. This applies both to the money that a person born on this day sometimes pours into his projects, having lost all sense of proportion, and to his health.

In other words, the Grain person needs to master such qualities as prudence and moderation, remembering that his capabilities, like the resources of his body, though very great, still have their limits. From time to time they need to be replenished, by giving not only the body but also the mind a rest.

In short, nature has been as generous to people of the Grain sign as it possibly could be. But for such mighty energy to bring the greatest benefit, they should keep a simple rule in mind: only that which is done in moderation is good.
