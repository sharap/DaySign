A person born on the day of the Eagle is a logician by nature. This is not about any special speed of thought: it is simply that his intellectual resources are vast, and in any question reason wins out over feelings and emotions. In fact, from the outside the Eagle often seems slower than other people, because he prefers to weigh the state of affairs properly first and only then settle on a decision. He does not like to rely on intuition and has no faith in sudden flashes of insight, but he can, as few others can, examine a situation thoughtfully and thoroughly before drawing a conclusion.

Thanks to this, the Eagle person's character turns out even and calm. Usually from childhood on he leaves the most pleasant impression on those around him: he dislikes bickering, is quite accommodating, and rarely gets into conflicts - first of all with parents and teachers, and later, in adult life, with his superiors. At the same time he not only does well in school but genuinely enjoys the process of learning itself. Unlike people of many other signs, studying comes to him without much effort.

The prudence typical of the Eagle often looks like weakness or indecision in the eyes of others. This is especially noticeable in the teenage years, when his peers are busy showing off youthful recklessness and maximalism. But this by no means implies that the Eagle is actually weak in spirit and ready to give way: his intellect simply tells him not to charge straight ahead but to calmly look for the paths that will lead to the desired result with the fewest losses.

In other words, if the Eagle has yielded to someone's crude pressure, do not assume he has admitted defeat. That impression is deceptive: for him a step back is merely a tactical move, he will never give up his interests, and at the first convenient opportunity he will do everything to turn the matter in his favor.

Those close to him should remember that the Eagle has an excellent memory and a cool head. Having retreated, he does not forget insults and is in no hurry to forgive those who caused them. Vindictiveness, generally speaking, is not in his nature, but any injury done to him remains an open question, and open questions are something he cannot stand. It happens that many years later he returns to an old conflict with a single purpose - to show his former opponents that the real winner was he.

Pride and ambition are traits highly characteristic of people of this sign. Quite often what matters to the Eagle is not so much winning as looking like the winner in the eyes of others.

In today's world the Eagle is a very favorable sign both for life and for a career. Logic, a sober view of things and inner balance help those born on this day find their place in the most varied fields: business, science, politics, the arts. They are endowed with a sense of humor, are not inclined to make mountains out of molehills, know how to be flexible and diplomatic, and at the same time possess the rare ability to find the best way out of their difficulties without unnecessary fuss.

Still, the Eagle's logical cast of mind has its downside. He often slips into excessive intellectualism, or even into obscurity, without caring much that his train of thought is accessible only to a few intellectuals like himself.

There is a catch, too, in the ease with which his mind helps him achieve his goals. It happens that, intoxicated by success, the Eagle begins to believe in his own omniscience and near omnipotence. In that state he is quite capable of going off the rails and making a great many sorry mistakes. Alcohol, casual affairs, gambling - all of these can quietly entangle the Eagle in nets that are not easy to escape afterwards.

Nor should it be forgotten that logic is by no means the only tool a person has. Rarely, perhaps, but life does bring situations in which the intellect proves completely powerless. Finding himself in unfamiliar surroundings where only instinct and intuition can be relied on, a person of logical bent is simply lost. This limitation of reason makes itself felt in matters of love, of death, and of life itself. All these realms are beyond the grasp of the logical mind, and when a problem touches on them, the Eagle risks becoming confused and tangled up. Then his sense of omnipotence may turn into a feeling of utter helplessness.

To overcome this limitation of reason, laid down by Nature itself, the Eagle must first of all admit that not logic alone but intuition, too, means a great deal in life. Without it many discoveries, and indeed all intellectual progress as a whole, would have been impossible. Intuition is the second wing, and once he has gained it, the Eagle, with his wisdom, keen sight and intelligence, will be able to soar to the very summit and reach unprecedented starry heights.
