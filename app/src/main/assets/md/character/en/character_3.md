Night is a remarkable sign in its own way. What sets apart a person born on this day, above all else, is how deeply he is absorbed in his own thoughts, feelings, worries and concerns. The sign gives a person strong pride, a vulnerable nature and a pull toward inner seclusion – and yet, paradoxically, in the modern world Night turns out to be one of the most favorable signs both for life and for a career.

That is exactly its contradiction. On the one hand, people of the Night have a rich imagination and the ability to concentrate; on the other, their habit of nursing their hurts inside themselves builds such valuable qualities as patience and persistence.

In other words, although the Maya books speak of a hard fate for those born on this day, the same books call them "hunters" – people who know how to get what they are after. Luck really is often on their side, but it is not blind luck: it comes from their ability to move toward a goal with patience and focus.

Perhaps the most noticeable trait of the Night person is his quiet self-reliance. Unlike many others, he rarely puts his independence on display. He simply goes about his own business, and everything else – including other people's opinions – interests him little. He cannot stand it when those around him try to pry into his soul, and he has no wish to pry into anyone else's.

A person born on this day knows how to keep himself in hand and keep other people's secrets, and he rarely shows what is really going on inside him. But behind that outward composure lies a great sensitivity to slights, and this should not be forgotten. Even if he gives nothing away, his grievances accumulate and grow to considerable strength, and he is not at all inclined to forgive and forget them.

From the outside, the Night person may seem open and even-tempered, but whatever impression he makes, his inner reserve always stays with him. He carefully guards his personal space and his own interests and puts them first in any undertaking. Those around him may take this for self-centeredness, but it is important to remember that the Night person treats the personal interests of others with the same respect. A vivid example is Abraham Lincoln, who freed the American slaves: he was born on a Night day.

Generally speaking, the diligence and powers of concentration typical of the Night person can hardly be counted as shortcomings. They are precisely what allow him to achieve serious success in almost any field. As a rule he moves toward his goal patiently, without getting distracted by trifles, stands up for his interests and knows how to wait. These qualities serve him well in climbing the career ladder, in running his own business and in creative work.

At the same time, people of the Night are very much given to daydreaming. The habit of living through problems alone develops the imagination, but touchiness often colors these fantasies in sad tones. They frequently feel that fate has been unfair to them, that they really deserve more and better, and this shows in their dreams and in their life as a whole. So the "Cinderella complex" is noticeably more common among people of the Night than among the other signs. Incidentally, the tale of Cinderella itself was written by the great storyteller Charles Perrault, who was born on a Night day.

This does not mean, however, that people of the Night get more than their share of hardships and hurts compared with everyone else. It is simply that by their very nature they feel them more deeply, and this is exactly where a serious danger lies for them.

First, the tendency to give their troubles excessive weight often means that even minor unpleasantness swells, in their eyes, to the scale of a cosmic tragedy. Second, having reached such a state, a person risks withdrawing into himself for good or turning bitter, laying the blame for his misfortunes on individual people, if not on all of humanity.

It might seem strange that under this sign nature itself seems to push a person deliberately toward all sorts of sorrows. What good can come of grievances? Yet there is a deep meaning in it: no one becomes a real human being in hothouse conditions, where everything runs smoothly and there are no difficulties. Night is a sign of trial, and whoever withstands it is capable of rising very high. Not for nothing do they say that the road to the stars leads through thorns.

Moreover, only the experience of one's own suffering can teach a person such priceless things as empathy and compassion.

One of the most striking examples of this sign's influence is the fate of the great seer Michel Nostradamus. For a long time the life of this man born under the Night sign consisted of privation and trials, until one day fate raised him to an unheard-of height, granting him the gift of prophecy.

Of course, far from everyone born on this day becomes a prophet. But the Night sign has a special feature: if a person endures all his trials with dignity and learns patience and empathy, then not only will fortune smile on him – nature itself may awaken in him remarkable abilities whose existence he may never even have suspected.
