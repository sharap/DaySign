The Sunrise person is perhaps the most exact embodiment of our modern civilization, with all its pluses and minuses. Wherever fate may cast him and whatever circumstances he finds himself in, he remains a modern man in the fullest sense of the words.

Sitting idle is not in his nature: Nature has arranged things so that the thirst for activity in his soul never subsides. It is so strong that if for some reason he cannot act on it, he is in danger of pessimism and even depression. Far more often, though, someone born on this day does find somewhere to direct his active nature.

The main traits of his character are probably practicality and concreteness. Abstract philosophical musings are not for the Sunrise person; he cannot stand uncertainty or unfinished business. His motto is "time is money", and it is by this formula that he tries to build his life.

Hence his perpetual search for the simplest and shortest roads to his goal. He dislikes complicating things: efficiency comes first for him, and for its sake he is ready to sacrifice a great deal.

The Sunrise person aims for the maximum result with the minimum investment, even if quality suffers. He has no ambition to leave a mark on the ages. But here is what is remarkable: although his ventures are far from always running like a Swiss watch, thanks to their simplicity and efficiency his work often looks no worse than the outstanding creations of great masters.

It is precisely these qualities that help the Sunrise person earn a living. He seems made to become an excellent professional in his field – after all, professionalism lies not in the ability to do the impossible but in the skill of making the best use of the resources already at hand. At the same time, it is extremely important to him to reach the result quickly and with the least expenditure of effort and money. He will not throw everything he has at reaching a goal: if a task demands excessive strain, he simply switches to another one and happily devotes the freed-up time to himself.

As for money, he could never be called a penny-pincher. Of course he is not against earning more, but he is quite capable of being content with what he has. Quite often people of this sign even spend more than they earn, live calmly on credit and do not worry too much about the future in that respect. Their creed fits into one simple thought: you only live once, so you have to make time to try everything.

The Sunrise person is also marked by independence and a wish to live by his own judgment. He loves personal freedom, values it and is ready to fight for it. Finding himself in a dependent position, he will not simply dream of freedom but will actively look for a concrete way out – and thanks to his practicality he will most likely find one.

In working life this usually looks like this: even while working for someone else, the Sunrise person will try to run his own affairs on the side – the way managers do in any large company. And if the opportunity arises, he will most likely open a business of his own, where the only boss is himself.

His own goals and interests always come first for the Sunrise person, and under any circumstances he is ready to defend them – decisively and toughly. There is no doubt about it: the famous "Nothing personal, it's just business" was invented by people of this very sign.

The independence and love of freedom of people of this sign show not only in the wish to live by their own judgment but also in free thinking. For the Sunrise person there are no forbidden topics. He is not in the habit of hiding his thoughts behind vague, flowery phrases and prefers to say straight out what he thinks; he can openly discuss any delicate subject, from politics to sex.

The flip side of this candor and directness is that conflicts often flare up around the Sunrise person. To those around him he sometimes seems too blunt: restraint and tact are not built into his sign. Keeping himself within the bounds of convention is not something the Sunrise person is used to.

Still, such a direct manner of dealing with people has weighty advantages too: with him nothing is left unsaid, he is predictable, and it is always clear what to expect from him. The Sunrise person may be used to seeing the world only in black and white, but one can say with complete confidence that you will never meet with Oriental guile from him.

At the same time, the Sunrise's urge to simplify has its dark side: the simplest way to escape a problem all too often turns out to be relieving the tension with alcohol or drugs. And the Sunrise person's independent temperament does not incline him to heed other people's experience or the opinions of those around him on this matter. So he should be especially careful that these dubious "simple pleasures" do not send his life off the rails.

The same simplicity, lack of inhibition and free thinking are fully characteristic of the Sunrise person in the sexual sphere as well. The energy of the sign does not incline him to restraint, and traditional family values are usually foreign to him. As a rule, the Sunrise person is an advocate of free relationships in love: he does not torment his partner with excessive jealousy and expects the same in return. For him marriage is above all a form of partnership, and everything else is secondary. That is exactly why people of the Sunrise sign most often choose a form of marital relations as frank and free of ambiguity as the prenuptial agreement.

On the whole, this sign suits both a career and life in general in today's world full of contradictions. After all, no one has absorbed the virtues and flaws of this world as fully as the Sunrise person, and here he feels like a fish in water.
