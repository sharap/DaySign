A man born under the sign of the World Tree, deeply attuned to the primal forces of Nature, carries within him an innate, almost instinctual connection to the living world. His intuition is his true compass—stronger than logic, more reliable than reason. He lives in a natural resonance with his environment, expressing his desires and emotions openly and honestly, often without filtering them through rational thought. This can make his behavior seem unpredictable or even childish to others—impulsive, passionate, and easily distracted—but his authenticity radiates a genuine charm that makes it hard to stay angry with him for long. Like a child who cries when hungry or uncomfortable, he speaks his needs directly, and this childlike honesty persists into adulthood. When he desires something—be it love, food, or creative expression—he pursues it with pure, unfiltered intent, not through calculation, but through instinct. Conversely, if he feels no desire, nothing can compel him to act. His interests may flare up quickly and fade just as fast, not from fickleness, but because his inner drive is satisfied, and only returns when the need re-emerges. He is passionate but forgiving, quick to anger, yet quicker to let go.

When at peace, he is serene, deeply attuned to nature, and content in quiet reflection. Yet beneath this calm lies a powerful, untamed energy—when deeply wounded or provoked, his emotional force erupts with sudden, overwhelming intensity. This is because his primary instincts are not just strong—they are the foundation of all his feelings, both noble and base. In moments of crisis, he reveals a hidden reservoir of strength, capable of extraordinary resilience and action, often surprising even himself. He is not built for routine or conformity; mundane duties feel like burdens, and he often struggles with the demands of everyday life. Yet this is where his true value lies: in times of crisis, he becomes a source of unexpected power, capable of solving problems that others cannot. Like the legendary Ilya Muromets, he may seem inactive for years, but when the world is in peril, he awakens with unstoppable force. His life may be marked by challenges, but these are not signs of bad karma—they are the very conditions that allow his inner power to emerge.

This man is highly sensitive to the emotional atmosphere around him, easily absorbing both joy and negativity. He is vulnerable to artificial comforts like alcohol or drugs, which can disrupt his natural balance. A polluted or chaotic environment drains him, while time spent in untouched nature restores his inner harmony. He is drawn to the accumulation of things—not for practicality, but for the thrill of possessing, even the seemingly useless. He collects memories, objects, and knowledge, often with no clear purpose—yet somehow, in the most unexpected moment, these "trinkets" become invaluable. He possesses a rare talent for transforming chaos into order, trash into treasure. In relationships, he is emotionally intense but inconsistent—he may ignore flaws for long periods, then suddenly demand perfection from others, oblivious to his own contradictions. Yet this inconsistency is not hypocrisy; it is the expression of a deeply intuitive, non-linear soul. He is a natural creator, thriving in artistic or visionary fields where intuition, depth, and authenticity matter more than logic. His life is not about comfort, but about readiness—prepared to rise when the world needs him most.
