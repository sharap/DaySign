The Jaguar is a person who knows how to get what is his. Nature was not stingy with him: it gave him a sharp, observant mind, decisiveness, and luck in achieving what he sets out to do.

Perhaps the chief trait of someone born on a Jaguar day is his concreteness. He cannot stand vague wording: when he says "yes" it always means "yes", and "no" always means "no". His emotions are fairly strong, yet his thinking cannot be called emotional. He is in command of his feelings, and his mind is sober and practical. As a rule this person is remarkably logical and consistent, and his judgments are invariably clear and precise. People of this sign often need little time to master a new field: they grasp everything on the fly and, most importantly, they do not merely catch the gist but also know how to put the knowledge they have gained to practical use.

So it is hardly surprising that three of the greatest geniuses in human history were born under this sign – Leonardo da Vinci, Isaac Newton and René Descartes. All of them were distinguished not only by a unique precision of judgment but also by the breadth of their interests.

Of course, one can hardly expect every person born under this sign to become a second Newton, but logic and a wide range of interests are more characteristic of Jaguar people than of anyone else.

The Jaguar person has a special faith in himself and his own abilities, and it does not come out of thin air. His ability to arrive quickly at well-founded, accurate conclusions gives him a right to his own opinion on all sorts of matters, including those where he could hardly be called an expert. Such confidence naturally draws people to him and helps the Jaguar succeed in whatever he takes on. It is no accident that the Maya considered this sign a lucky one.

The Jaguar knows how to work and is used to seeing things through to the end; a case in point is the life and career of the American industrialist Henry Ford, who was born on this day. His name, which became a car brand known all over the world, still serves as a symbol of American practicality and success. There is nothing superfluous in his cars, but they have reliability, an austere beauty, and the best combination of price and quality.

Other Jaguars organize their lives just as sensibly: they do not chase excessive luxury and comfort, they are not afraid of hard work, and they are able to put their business on a firm footing, getting results where others would be ready to give up ten times over. On this path they are greatly helped by their innate practicality and concreteness, together with a sharp mind and an unshakable belief in their own powers.

Thanks to his natural curiosity, the Jaguar is very open to everything new, strives to keep up with the times, and never looks old-fashioned. This applies first of all to his special interest in technical novelties and achievements: it is in technology that the Jaguar finds the very precision and practicality he always strives for himself.

The same love of precision shows in the fact that the Jaguar has an organic intolerance of lies and deceit. A lie is a deliberate distortion of reality that breaks logic, and for the Jaguar, with his craving for precision and concreteness, coming to terms with that is simply impossible.

Another striking feature of the Jaguar is his heightened physical activity. He loves his body, loves to put it under strain, and passive rest is alien to his temperament. As a rule, Jaguars are very athletic and energetic people, so they have room to apply their talents not only in science or business but also in sport. What is more, they often manage to make their mark in several fields at once! Thus Arnold Schwarzenegger, the most famous bodybuilder on the planet, was born on a Jaguar day, and his passion for the culture of the body did not in the least prevent him from applying his sharp mind to politics – as governor of California, the richest state in America.

That said, one cannot claim the Jaguar is entirely without faults. Because of his quick, concrete mind, his judgments often sound too harsh, and those around him take them as categorical, if not peremptory. From the outside the Jaguar sometimes comes across as a domineering person who overpowers others – especially subordinates or those close to him. For the sake of a quiet life, of course, he would do well to soften this trait by learning to be gentler and more tolerant. But it is also useful for those around him to remember that the Jaguar's apparent peremptoriness is for the most part only an appearance. It would be wrong to think that other people's opinions mean nothing to him: he will gladly listen to someone else's arguments, provided they are well-founded, logical and concrete.

All in all, the Jaguar (especially if he manages to soften his characteristic harshness and authoritarianism) is a very fortunate sign, both for life and for a career. Such a person is destined to live an interesting, full life, and his energy, sober observant mind and innate practicality will always secure him a worthy place in this world.
