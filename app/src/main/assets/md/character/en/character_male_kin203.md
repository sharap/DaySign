His ideal world is one where truth, justice, and inner peace coexist — a vision that drives him toward creative, intellectual, or humanitarian pursuits. He thrives in environments that allow for deep work, personal growth, and meaningful contribution, whether as an artist, thinker, innovator, or leader.
